This page tracks progress toward climbing the 82 official 4000-meter summits of the Alps as defined by the International Climbing and Mountaineering Federation (UIAA).

Reaching 4000 meters represents the threshold for high-alpine mountaineering, requiring skills like crampon technique, glacier navigation, and crevasse rescue. Scaling all 82 summits is one of the ultimate grand challenges in European alpinism, spanning Mont Blanc, Monte Rosa, the Bernese Alps, and the Valais.
