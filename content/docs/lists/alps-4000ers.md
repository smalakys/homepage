This page tracks progress toward climbing the 82 official 4000-meter summits of the Alps as defined by the International Climbing and Mountaineering Federation (UIAA).

Reaching 4000 meters is the definitive threshold for high-alpine mountaineering, requiring skills like crampon technique, glacier navigation, and crevasse rescue. The UIAA's official list encompasses 82 independent peaks across France, Switzerland, and Italy. Scaling all summits is a celebrated grand challenge in European alpinism.
