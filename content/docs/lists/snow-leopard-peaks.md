This page tracks progress toward climbing the five 7000-meter peaks required to earn the historic Snow Leopard mountaineering award.

Established in 1967 in the former Soviet Union, the Snow Leopard award is a celebrated honour recognizing ascents of all five 7000-meter summits across the Pamir and Tian Shan ranges. Though no longer officially awarded, it remains a prestigious objective for expedition mountaineers seeking the ultimate test of high-altitude endurance in Central Asia.
