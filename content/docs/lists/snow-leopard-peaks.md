This page tracks progress toward climbing the five 7000-meter peaks required to earn the historic Snow Leopard mountaineering award.

Established in 1967, the Snow Leopard award is one of high-altitude mountaineering's most prestigious honours. The five 7000-meter summits span the Pamir and Tian Shan ranges: Ismoil Somoni Peak (7495 m), Jengish Chokusu (7439 m), Ibn Sina Peak (7134 m), Ozodi Peak (7105 m), and Khan Tengri (7010 m). These mountains present extreme high-altitude environments with intense storms, glaciated terrain, and avalanche risks.
