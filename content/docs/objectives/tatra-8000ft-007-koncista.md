## Description

Končistá is a broad, high summit on the ridge above Mengusovská and Batizovská valleys. Its long southern ridge gives it a commanding position in the central High Tatras.

The mountain is approached from Popradské pleso or the Sliezsky dom side and climbed by guided mountaineering routes. Its upper ridge is not a marked tourist path.
