## Description

Dent Blanche is an isolated, four-ridged pyramid above the Val d'Hérens and Zinal in the Swiss Pennine Alps. Its scale and symmetrical profile make it a major objective despite its separation from the highest massifs.

The normal route follows the south ridge from the Cabane de la Dent Blanche. The north face and the other ridges are substantially more serious lines.
