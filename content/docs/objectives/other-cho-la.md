## Description

Cho La is the high pass linking the Gokyo valley with the Everest side of Khumbu. It is one of the three principal passes on the Three Passes Trek.

The usual crossing runs between Dzongla and Thagnak, with a glaciated, boulder-strewn eastern side. It is a trekking pass, but snow and route-finding can make the crossing consequential.
