## Description

Punta Baretti is a remote summit on Mont Blanc's Italian south side, on the Brouillard–Innominata ridge system. It is associated with serious, seldom-visited high routes rather than a routine normal ascent.

Rifugio Monzino and Bivacco Eccles provide the usual bases. Ridge itineraries through the Innominata and Brouillard sectors are the peak's principal mountaineering context.
