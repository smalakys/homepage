Jengish Chokusu, formerly Pobeda Peak, is one of the five Snow Leopard peaks and the highest summit of the Tian Shan. Routes approach through the Inylchek Glacier system and involve long, technical mixed ridges at extreme altitude. Severe weather, isolation, and objective hazards make this one of the most demanding peaks on the list.
