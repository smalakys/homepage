## Description

Kernavė – Vilnius Ultra is a point-to-point trail race from Kernavė, Lithuania's 13th-century capital, to Vilnius, the current one. It has been held since 2015.

The two cities are about 35 km apart in a straight line, but the route follows the bends of the Neris and the surrounding terrain to make the main distance of around 80 km. About 80% of it runs through forest, the rest along the riverbank, meadows and small settlements, with short climbs and stream valleys.
