## Description

Bieggavákgáisá is a remote summit in southern Porsanger, Finnmark, on the boundary of Stabbursdalen National Park. It is among the higher peaks of the Gáisá landscape.

There is no widely documented waymarked summit route; an ascent is an off-trail navigation outing in protected Arctic terrain rather than a standardised alpine climb.
