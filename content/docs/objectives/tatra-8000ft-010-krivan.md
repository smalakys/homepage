## Description

Kriváň is one of Slovakia's national symbols, distinguished by its bent summit ridge above the western High Tatras. It is a prominent hiking mountain with extensive views.

Marked routes reach the summit from Tri studničky or from the Štrbské Pleso side. The final ridge involves straightforward scrambling but no technical climbing in normal summer conditions.
