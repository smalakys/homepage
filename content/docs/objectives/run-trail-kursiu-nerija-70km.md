## Description

Trail Kuršių Nerija (#TKN) is an October trail race on the Curonian Spit, the UNESCO-listed sand peninsula between the Baltic Sea and the Curonian Lagoon. Its distances range from a 1 km children's run to over 100 km.

The 70 km starts at the Parnidis dune in Nida. There is plenty of sand underfoot, along with forest roads and stretches by the sea.
