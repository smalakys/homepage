## Description

Kozi Wierch is the highest summit lying wholly in the Polish Tatras. It sits on the Orla Perć ridge above the Valley of Five Polish Lakes.

Marked routes approach from the Valley of Five Polish Lakes or from Murowaniec via Zawrat. The Orla Perć is the notable exposed ridge traverse through this section of the High Tatras.
