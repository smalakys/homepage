## Description

Čohkarášša is the highest mountain in Porsanger municipality, in the Gáisá country of Finnmark. It stands within Stabbursdalen National Park and is a significant regional summit.

The peak is reached as a remote off-trail objective rather than by a formal hut-to-hut or marked summit route. Respect for the park and competent navigation are central to planning an ascent.
