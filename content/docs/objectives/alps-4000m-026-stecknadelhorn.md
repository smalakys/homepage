## Description

Stecknadelhorn is a narrow summit on the Nadelgrat between Nadelhorn and Hohberghorn. Its value is as an exposed link in one of the Pennine Alps' classic high traverses.

The Mischabel Hut is the standard base. It is normally climbed on the Nadelgrat, either from Nadelhorn or from the Hohberghorn–Dürrenhorn side.
