## Description

The Dom is the highest summit entirely in Switzerland and the culminating peak of the Mischabel group above Randa. Its glaciated north-west flank contrasts with the long rocky ridges that define the massif.

The normal route is staged at the Domhütte and reaches the summit by the Festijoch and north-west ridge. The Festigrat and the Hohberggraat are celebrated, much more sustained alternatives.
