## Description

Slavkovský Štít is a broad summit above Starý Smokovec, with a long south-east ridge that makes it one of the most accessible high Tatra peaks. It is a fine viewpoint toward the central range.

A marked trail climbs directly from Starý Smokovec over the long ridge to the summit. The route is strenuous but does not require technical climbing in summer conditions.
