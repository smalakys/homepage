## Description

Allalinhorn is a prominent glaciated summit above Saas-Fee, readily reached from the Mittelallalin lift station but still a genuine high-alpine objective. It is also a major ski-mountaineering summit.

The normal route crosses the upper glacier from Mittelallalin. The Hohlaubgrat from the Britannia Hut is the classic more independent ridge alternative.
