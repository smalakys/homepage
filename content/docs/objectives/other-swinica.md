## Description

Świnica is a prominent summit in the Polish High Tatras, near Kasprowy Wierch. It is a classic exposed mountain hike above the Zakopane side.

Marked routes reach the summit from Zawrat or Kasprowy Wierch. The Orla Perć intersects the wider ridge system and makes a demanding continuation for experienced hikers.
