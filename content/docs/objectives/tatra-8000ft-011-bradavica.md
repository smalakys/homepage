## Description

Bradavica is a complex of granite summits above the Velická and Batizovská valleys in the central High Tatras. Its ridges and faces make it a mountaineering, rather than tourist-trail, objective.

Approaches are normally made from Sliezsky dom, followed by guided climbing routes through the upper basins and ridges. Route selection depends on the chosen summit and conditions.
