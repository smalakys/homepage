Ismoil Somoni Peak, formerly Communism Peak, is one of the five Snow Leopard peaks and the highest mountain in Tajikistan. Expeditions generally approach through the Pamir glaciers and use a chain of high camps before attempting the summit ridge. Its altitude, remoteness, and weather make it a serious expedition objective.
