## Description

Corne du Diable is the south-western spire of the Aiguilles du Diable on Mont Blanc du Tacul. It is the usual first summit of the five-spire traverse.

The Arête du Diable is normally approached from the Refuge des Cosmiques through the Col du Diable, then crosses Chaubert, Médiane, Carmen and L'Isolée toward Tacul. The route is a classic high-altitude granite traverse.
