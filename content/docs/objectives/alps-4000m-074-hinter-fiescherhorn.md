## Description

Hinter Fiescherhorn is the eastern summit of the Fiescherhorn group above the Aletsch Glacier. It is normally climbed as part of a traverse with Gross Fiescherhorn.

The Mönchsjoch Hut is the usual base, with access over the glacier and the Fieschersattel. The connecting ridge gives the outing its principal alpine interest.
