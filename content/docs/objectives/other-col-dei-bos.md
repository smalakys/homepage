## Description

Col dei Bos is a prominent Dolomite summit above Passo Falzarego, opposite the Tofane. Its pale rock walls and proximity to the First World War landscape give it an important local climbing context.

Approaches normally start from Passo Falzarego. The summit is reached by steep paths and scrambling terrain, while the surrounding faces carry established Dolomite rock routes.
