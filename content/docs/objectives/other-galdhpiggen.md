Galdhøpiggen is the highest summit in Norway and Northern Europe. Common approaches start from Juvasshytta, crossing the Styggebreen with a guide, or from Spiterstulen on a longer route over the surrounding ridges. Both routes involve exposed, changeable mountain conditions near the summit.
