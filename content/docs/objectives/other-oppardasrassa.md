## Description

Oppardasrášša is a summit in the Porsanger Gáisá region of Finnmark. It is a remote Arctic mountain rather than a developed climbing destination.

Available route information is limited; an ascent should be treated as an off-trail navigation outing in open tundra terrain, with local land-use and weather considerations setting the character of the day.
