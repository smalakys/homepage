## Description

Pyšný Štít is a sharp High Tatras summit on the ridge above Malá Studená dolina, close to Baranie rohy. It is a mountaineering summit rather than a marked hiking destination.

Téryho chata is the usual base for approaches through the valley. The final ridge is reached on guided climbing routes in the Baranie rohy sector.
