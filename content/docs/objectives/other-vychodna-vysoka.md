## Description

Východná Vysoká is a sharp High Tatras summit above the Velická and Zlomisková valleys. Its position gives it a fine view toward Gerlachovský štít and the central Tatra ridges.

The usual mountaineering approach comes from Sliezsky dom through the Polský hrebeň area. The final summit terrain is a guided climbing route rather than a marked tourist trail.
