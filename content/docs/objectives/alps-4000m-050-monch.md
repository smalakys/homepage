Mönch is a prominent Bernese Alps summit between the Jungfrau and Eiger. Its normal route starts near the Mönchsjochhütte and follows the southeast ridge, a sustained snow and mixed climb with exposed sections. It is often combined with other objectives in the Jungfrau region.
