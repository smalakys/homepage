## Description

Baranie rohy is a prominent twin-topped High Tatras summit above Malá Studená dolina. It is one of the principal granite objectives reached from Téryho chata.

The normal guided route approaches via the upper valley and Baranie saddle, then follows exposed scrambling and climbing terrain to the summit. It lies beyond the marked tourist network.
