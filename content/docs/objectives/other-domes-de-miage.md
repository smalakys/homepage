## Description

The Dômes de Miage are a chain of snowy summits above the Contamines valley on the French side of the Mont Blanc Group. Their long ridge is a classic high-alpine traverse.

The Refuge des Conscrits is the usual base. The normal traverse follows the domes' snow crest and can be extended toward Aiguille de Bionnassay.
