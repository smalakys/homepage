Dent du Géant is a distinctive granite tooth in the Mont Blanc Group. The usual approach from the Torino Hut crosses the Géant Glacier and reaches the summit by steep rock and fixed-rope climbing on the southwest side. Its short-looking profile belies a technical and exposed ascent.
