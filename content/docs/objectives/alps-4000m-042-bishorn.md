## Description

Bishorn is the northern summit of the Weisshorn massif above the Val d'Anniviers. Its broad upper snow slopes offer a contrast to the sharp Weisshorn ridge beyond.

The normal route is based at the Cabane de Tracuit and follows the north-west ridge. The traverse over the Weisshorn is a far more technical continuation.
