Ibn Sina Peak, formerly Lenin Peak, is one of the five Snow Leopard peaks. The standard approach uses camps on the Kyrgyz side of the Alay Range and follows broad glacier and snow slopes toward the summit. High altitude, crevasses, and rapidly changing weather remain central considerations.
