## Description

Lastoi de Formin is a rugged Dolomite summit above Passo Giau and the Mondeval basin. Its limestone towers form part of the wild ridge south of the Croda da Lago group.

Approaches normally start at Passo Giau or from the Mondeval side. The summit is a scrambling and route-finding objective, while its walls offer traditional Dolomite climbing terrain.
