## Description

The Weisshorn is one of the most distinctive pyramids in the Pennine Alps, rising above Zinal, Randa and the Turtmann valley. Its three great ridges and striking faces make it a defining Swiss alpine summit.

The normal route follows the north ridge from the Weisshorn Hut. The Schali ridge and east ridge are major alternatives, while the north face is a historic ice and mixed climb.
