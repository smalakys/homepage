## Description

Ozodi Peak, formerly Korzhenevskaya Peak, is one of Tajikistan's five Snow Leopard summits in the Pamirs. It is normally climbed from the shared Moskvina Meadow base-camp area.

The standard ascent follows the peak's north-west ridge through established high camps. It is a substantial glacier-and-snow expedition and is often paired logistically with Ismoil Somoni Peak.
