Ozodi Peak, formerly Korzhenevskaya Peak, is one of the five Snow Leopard peaks. The usual expedition approach follows the Pamir glacier systems to a series of high camps and then a long summit ridge. Altitude, remoteness, and weather are significant parts of the route’s difficulty.
