## Description

Ludwigshöhe is a summit on Monte Rosa's Italian–Swiss crest, above the Lys Glacier. It is most naturally visited during a tour of the high ridge around Signalkuppe.

The normal approach begins at Rifugio Gnifetti or Rifugio Mantova and crosses the Lys Glacier. A ridge traverse can include Parrotspitze and Corno Nero in the same outing.
