## Description

Vilnius Trail Run is a trail running event in Vilnius. Its longest route, the 108+ km ultra, follows the Takas aplink Vilnių ("Trail around Vilnius"), a marked hiking trail that circles the city.

The trail runs through the Pavilniai and Verkiai regional parks, mostly on forest and natural paths, with stretches of city streets in between. The race is certified as a UTMB Index 100K race with ITRA 4 and the IAU Trail Label.
