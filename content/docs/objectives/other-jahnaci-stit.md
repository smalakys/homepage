## Description

Jahňací štít is the easternmost major summit of the High Tatras, rising above the Valley of Green Lake. It is a conspicuous granite peak at the end of the main range.

The standard approach begins at Chata pri Zelenom plese and follows the marked route through steep, exposed terrain. The surrounding walls also hold traditional Tatra climbing lines.
