## Description

Piz Boè is the highest summit of the Sella Group and one of the most accessible high points in the Dolomites. Capanna Fassa stands immediately below its summit.

The normal routes start from Sass Pordoi or the Passo Pordoi side and cross the Sella plateau on steep, sometimes equipped paths. The summit is a natural centrepiece of Sella traverses.
