## Description

Piz Bernina is the highest summit of the Eastern Alps and the crown of the Bernina Group. Its famous Biancograt is among the great snow ridges of the Alps.

The normal route follows the Spalla ridge from Rifugio Marco e Rosa. The Biancograt from the Tschierva Hut is the classic long and technical alternative.
