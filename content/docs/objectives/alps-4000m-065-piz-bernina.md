Piz Bernina is the highest summit in the Eastern Alps. The normal route approaches from the Rifugio Marco e Rosa over the upper glacier and south ridge; the celebrated Biancograt is a separate, more technical north-ridge line. The first ascent was made on 13 September 1850 by Johann Coaz with Jon and Lorenz Ragut Tscharner.
